LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made between the Landlord and the Tenant for the property located at 45 Lake View Road.

1. Rent shall be Rs. 30,000 payable monthly in advance on the 1st of each month.

2. The tenant shall pay a security deposit of 2 months rent at the time of signing this agreement.

3. Either party may terminate this lease by providing 60 days written notice to the other party.

4. The landlord shall be responsible for all structural repairs and maintenance of the premises, while the tenant is responsible for routine upkeep.

5. The security deposit shall be returned to the tenant within 30 days of vacating the premises, less any deductions for damages.

6. This lease may be renewed for an additional term upon mutual written agreement of both parties at least 30 days before expiry.
