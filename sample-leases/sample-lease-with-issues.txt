LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made between the Landlord and the Tenant for the property located at 12 Green Avenue.

1. Rent shall be Rs. 25,000 payable monthly in advance on the 5th of each month.

2. The tenant shall pay a security deposit of 5 months rent at the time of signing this agreement.

3. This lease shall automatically renew for successive one year terms unless either party objects in writing.

4. In the event of termination, the party terminating this agreement must provide 10 days notice in writing to the other party.

5. The tenant agrees that the security deposit is non-refundable under any circumstances.

6. The tenant waives all rights to legal action against the landlord for any dispute arising under this lease.
