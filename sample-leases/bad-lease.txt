RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is between the Landlord and the Tenant.

1. RENT
The monthly rent is INR 20,000.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equivalent to 5 months' rent.
The deposit is non-refundable under all circumstances.

3. NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this lease by providing 15 days' written notice.

4. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance and repairs.

5. DEPOSIT RETURN
The deposit will be returned after the Tenant vacates the property.

6. RENEWAL
This lease shall automatically renew for another one-year period unless
the Landlord decides otherwise.

7. RENT INCREASE
The Landlord may increase the rent at any time and in its sole discretion
without prior notice.

8. LEGAL RIGHTS
The Tenant irrevocably waives all rights to take legal action against
the Landlord.

9. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate the agreement at any time.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
